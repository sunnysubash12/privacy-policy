Name


Email Address


Job Title


Company Name


Company Size


Industry


Social Media URLs


Lead Score and CRM Tags


IP Address (if captured by webhook)



2. How We Use Your Information
The information we collect is used for the following purposes:
To process and route leads to the appropriate sales or CRM system


To enrich leads using third-party tools (e.g., Clearbit, Apollo)


To evaluate and score leads using AI services (e.g., OpenAI)


To notify teams via Slack, Email, or other integrated services


To store or log activity in tools like Google Sheets or Airtable


To analyze performance and improve lead qualification strategies



3. Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell or rent your personal data. However, we may share data with the following services only for operational purposes:
CRM platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive)


Data enrichment APIs (e.g., Clearbit, Apollo, OpenAI)


Communication tools (e.g., Slack, Gmail)


Data storage services (e.g., Google Sheets, Airtable)


Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Data Retention
We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law.

5. Your Rights
